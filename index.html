NetPulse Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2 October 2026

Summary: NetPulse is a data-usage monitor for Android. Your usage statistics are read and stored on your device only. NetPulse has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics, and it does not send your usage data to the developer or to anyone else.

Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the NetPulse Android app (package com.aistudio.netpulse.kpzvrq), published by Yash Kavaiya ("the developer").

Information the app accesses

InformationWhyWhere it goes
Network usage statistics per app (bytes sent and received over mobile data and Wi-Fi)To show your usage, history, plan progress and tipsStays on your device
Names and icons of installed appsTo label usage in the app listStays on your device
Your data plan settings and preferencesTo track your billing cycle and send alertsStays on your device (see "Backups")
Speed test resultsTo show your result historyStays on your device

NetPulse does not read the content of your network traffic, your browsing history, messages, contacts, location, photos or files.

Usage access permission

Android only provides per-app network statistics to apps that you grant "Usage access" in system settings. NetPulse uses this permission solely to read network usage counters. You can revoke it at any time in Android Settings; the app will then stop showing usage.

Information shared with third parties

NetPulse does not sell or share your personal information.

The only time the app communicates over the internet is when you start a speed test. The test exchanges randomly generated test data with Cloudflare's speed test servers (speed.cloudflare.com). As with any internet connection, Cloudflare can see your IP address during the test. No usage statistics or app information are sent. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Other permissions

  • Notifications: to send data-plan alerts.
  • Internet and network state: to run the speed test and detect whether you are on Wi-Fi or mobile data.

Backups

If Android's device backup is enabled on your phone, your data plan settings and app preferences may be included in the backup that Android stores in your own Google account. This is managed by Android and Google, not by the developer, and the developer has no access to it.

Exports

If you use the export or backup features, the app writes a file to the location you choose. What happens to that file afterwards is under your control.

Retention and deletion

All app data is kept on your device until you delete it. You can remove it at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings or by uninstalling NetPulse. Because the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing to request or delete on a server.

Children

NetPulse is a general utility and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this page will be updated and the effective date revised.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer email shown on the app's Google Play listing, or raised at github.com/Yash-Kavaiya/NetPulse-app/issues.
